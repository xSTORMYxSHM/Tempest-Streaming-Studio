REVIEWER + BROADCASTER TEST

Verify the panel in under two minutes

  1. Activate Tempest Mainframe in a Twitch Panel slot and open the channel page.
  2. Open the panel and confirm the header reports that Studio is online.
  3. Confirm Current Stream and any enabled informational modules display their latest Studio data.
  4. Choose an enabled free interaction, poll, counter, command, or dice control and confirm the panel reports the result.
  5. If identity is required for a restricted action or one-vote poll, select Share Twitch Identity and repeat the action.

Expected offline state: Mainframe still loads when Studio is unavailable, identifies the connection state, and keeps unavailable live controls disabled until Studio reconnects. No additional viewer account or test credentials are required.
